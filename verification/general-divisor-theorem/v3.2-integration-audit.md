# v3.2 Integration Audit: Draft v3.1 vs. the Audited Theorem Chain

Draft v3.1 (`mb_paper.pdf` / `goodmath.app/paper.pdf`) compared section by section against the
now-closed chain: Theorem 1 ⊂ Theorem 1′ ⊂ Theorem 2 ⊂ Theorem 3, as verified in
`specialization-audit-repaired.md`. **Draft v3.1 is not edited in this pass.** This is the
complete change surface only.

**Headline finding, stated up front because it shapes every row below: nothing in Draft v3.1 is
mathematically wrong.** Theorem 1 was independently confirmed as an *exact* corollary of Theorem
3 (`specialization-audit.md`, `specialization-audit-repaired.md`) — same period, same count, same
correction factor, no discrepancy. Every change identified below is additive (new sections,
forward references, notational unification) or clarifying (making explicit that a hypothesis
happens to coincide with the sharp one in this special case) — not corrective. That distinction
matters for how v3.2 should be framed: it's an extension of a correct result, not a fix.
